\section{Introduction}

This report presents the results of the ET4ICP IC-Technology Practical Course and studies how the BICMOS5 fabrication process determines the final electrical behaviour of integrated-circuit test structures and MOS transistors. The work combines analytical calculations, process and device simulations, cleanroom processing assignments, and final electrical measurements on fabricated wafer structures.

The practical course is centered on the educational BICMOS5 process, in which NMOS and PMOS devices are formed together with process control module (PCM) test structures for monitoring the technological steps. Besides the transistors themselves, the wafer contains dedicated structures such as Van der Pauw resistors and electrical linewidth measurement structures, which make it possible to extract sheet resistance, doping-related quantities, and lateral diffusion effects from the finished process.

The main goal of this report is to document the assignments of the course and to compare results obtained from theory, Sentaurus simulations, processing observations, and electrical measurements. In particular, the report relates earlier calculations and simulations of junction depth, implantation behaviour, and threshold-voltage adjustment to later experimental results for sheet resistance, dopant concentration, transistor threshold voltage, mobility, and short-channel effects.

The report is organized as follows. Chapter 2 presents the technology assignments and analytical calculations based on the BICMOS5 process flow. Chapter 3 discusses the process simulations, including implantation, diffusion, channeling, and masking behaviour. Chapter 4 covers the device simulations of NMOS and PMOS structures, with emphasis on junction depths, threshold voltage, and current--voltage characteristics. Chapter 5 presents the processing assignments, including doping concentration extraction, oxide deposition and etching, and aluminium patterning. Chapter 6 reports the electrical measurements on the fabricated BiCMOS5 wafer, including Van der Pauw, ELM, and transistor characterization measurements. Finally, Chapter 7 compares the overall trends and discusses the agreement and remaining differences between analytical models, simulations, processing results, and measurements.
